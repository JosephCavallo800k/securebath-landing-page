The Difference Is In The Entry

Why step in when you can sit in?

A low-step walk-in tub can make getting into the bath easier today. But what happens if your mobility changes in the years ahead?

That's the question behind the Secure Bath approach. Instead of making the step the center of the solution, we designed around a different idea: sit down first, then bring your legs inside.

When you're making an investment in bathroom safety, it makes sense to think about how that solution needs to work for you over time — not just today.

Why Secure Bath Exists

We learned something from years in this industry.

Before starting Secure Bath, we spent years in the walk-in-tub industry — including nine years with Safe Step.

Over time, customers called back after making their investment and told us they could no longer get into their tub because the step had become too difficult for them.

That experience stayed with us.

If stepping is the problem, why build the solution around stepping? That's the question that led us to focus on the sit-down-first approach.
